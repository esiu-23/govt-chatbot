Who Controls Chicago?

Analysis 1 · Decision-makers, spending, and accountability in city government

Chicago's government has two kinds of leaders: those you elect, and those they appoint. This analysis maps who holds decision-making power, how much money flows through their control, and — where the data allows — who put them there. Where information is not available in the Chicago Open Data portal, fields are left blank.
