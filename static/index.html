About This Tool

This is a helper tool that answers questions about City of Chicago services. It covers city departments, Chicago Public Schools, and the Chicago Park District. Note this is not an official city tool.

All answers come from chicago.gov, cps.edu, and chicagoparkdistrict.com. Think of it as a smart search through city websites.

Data notice: This tool uses limited saved snapshots of chicago.gov, cps.edu, and chicagoparkdistrict.com as of 4/11/26. The answers show the date when the information was last collected. For the most current details, follow the source links in each answer.

How to Use It

  1. Type your question in plain English — no special words needed.
  2. Hit Send or press Enter.
  3. Read the answer and check the source links if you want to learn more.
  4. Ask follow-up questions any time!

What It Can't Do

  • It cannot access your personal city accounts or records.
  • It cannot process payments or applications.
  • It cannot answer questions unrelated to Chicago city services, schools, or parks.
  • It may not have the very latest updates — always verify with the official site for important matters.
