Four tools to help you understand and engage with your city government.

Know Your Block

Enter your address and see what the city is doing nearby — new business licenses, building permits, TIF investments, recent crimes and crashes, food inspection results, and open 311 requests.

Explore City Services

Ask questions about what services the City of Chicago offers — from parks and schools to permits and social services. Get plain-language answers drawn from official city sources.

Search & Browse Legislation

Find ordinances, resolutions, and other matters introduced to the Chicago City Council. Search in plain language and see the full legislative timeline for any bill.

Block Brief — Weekly Neighborhood Newsletter

Subscribe to a free weekly email digest for your block. Every Monday: crimes and crashes, restaurant inspection results, new business openings and closings, building permits, 311 requests, and park events — all pulled from real Chicago Open Data, filtered to within a few blocks of your address.
